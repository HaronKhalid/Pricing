Questions, answered

How long does a website take?

Usually 3 to 5 working days, once you have sent all your content and feedback. Complex projects can take longer.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo, phone and WhatsApp number, address, a short description, services, photos and social media links. We organise and present everything professionally.

Can I start small and add services later?

Yes. Many clients start with a website and add Google Business Profile, social media or reels later.

Can the package fit my budget?

We adjust the package to your budget by choosing which services and features to include.

Is the advertising budget included?

No. Advertising budget is separate from management fees and goes directly to the ad platform.

Do you guarantee rankings or sales?

No. SEO results and search rankings cannot be guaranteed, and we do not promise followers, leads or sales. We guarantee the agreed work and deliverables.

What about SEO, ads and automation?

These are quoted individually based on your website, business and goals. Message us for a custom quote.
